# Plant Brain

> **When your best technician retires, their knowledge doesn't.**

Plant Brain is a maintenance memory for a factory, built entirely on Snowflake for the **Snowflake CoCo CLI Hackathon 2026 – GCC Edition** (Track 3: Predictive Maintenance & OEE Command Center). It passively turns messy work orders and shift handover notes into structured knowledge cards. It connects those cards to live sensor anomalies, estimates a failure window, and drafts a work order with the known fix, citing its sources. A human approves the work order, and every closed job becomes new knowledge.

> ⚠️ **SYNTHETIC DATA.** The plant ("Deccan Precision Components, Pune"), its people, assets, and every number in this repo are fictional and generated by `data_gen/generate.py` (seed=42).
